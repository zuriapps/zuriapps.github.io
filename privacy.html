Privacy Policy

Zuri Apps Limited (“we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy. This policy explains how the Glaze app handles information.

Information you create

Glaze lets you create journal entries, reflections, emotions, photos, and related content. This content is stored on your device and, when iCloud sync is enabled, in your private Apple iCloud account. We do not operate a server that receives or reads this journal content.

Purchases

If you make an in-app purchase, Apple processes the payment. We use RevenueCat to help manage subscription access and purchase status. RevenueCat may process limited purchase-related and device information as described in its privacy policy.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Glaze schedules reminders locally on your device. You can turn them off in the app or in iOS Settings.

Photos

Photos are only accessed when you choose to add them to an entry. They are stored with your app content on your device and, if enabled, in your private iCloud account.

Your choices

You can delete entries in the app and manage iCloud data through your Apple account. You can manage purchases through your Apple ID subscription settings.

Contact

For privacy questions, email support@zuriapps.com.

Changes

We may update this policy when the app changes. We will post the updated version here and revise the date above.
